    What Hormozi actually watches

    LTV in gross profit, never revenue
    Lifetime value = money left after COGS, shipping and processing. Revenue LTV flatters broke companies.
    LTV : CAC of at least 3 : 1
    His stated floor. Below it, fix the machine before buying more traffic; his portfolio aims far higher.
    30-day cash — Client-Financed Acquisition
    The $100M Leads bar: recover what it costs to acquire and fulfill a customer within 30 days — one card cycle. His money-model rule raises it to 2×: collect double (CAC + fulfillment) in 30 days, so customer #1 funds the next two and you scale without outside capital.
    Payback period in months
    The faster an ad dollar comes home, the more times you can respin it in a year. Velocity beats margin.
    Absolute dollars
    You deposit dollars, not percentages. He optimizes gross-profit dollars per customer per month.

    What he ignores

    Top-line revenue bragging
    A big number with no margin behind it changes nothing about what you can spend.
    Brand awareness, impressions, followers
    Only interesting the day they measurably lower CAC or raise LTV.
    CPL, CPM, CTR in isolation
    Inputs, not outcomes. The only two numbers that make decisions are CAC and payback.
    Profitable "on paper"
    Accounting profit with a 90-day cash lag still kills the company. Cash timing outranks the P&L.
    Fear of losing on the first sale
    A front-end loss is fine when the payback window is short and the LTV deep — that fear is exactly what keeps competitors' budgets small.
    Model notes & formulas
    • Gross profit / order = AOV − COGS (AOV ÷ ratio) − shipping − warehouse fee − processing.
    • 30-day GP / customer = 30-day revenue multiple × gross profit per order (AOV held constant, so revenue multiples equal order multiples).
    • Max CAC (break even in 30 d) = 30-day GP — recovering acquisition + fulfillment inside one card cycle, the $100M Leads client-financed bar.
    • Self-funding target = the CAC where 30-day cash collected ≥ 2 × (CAC + cost to fulfill) — his 2× money-model rule; algebraically, 30-day GP − half of 30-day revenue.
    • Months 2–12 follow a geometric repeat falloff, normalized so the 12-month total exactly equals the 12-month LTV multiple.
    • Simulator: month-1 budget buys cohort #1; month m spends reinvest-% × month m−1 gross profit (optionally never below the month-1 budget). Each cohort replays the per-customer curve from its own start month.
    • Ratios follow Hormozi's published usage: LTV as gross profit and the 3:1 floor ($100M Leads), Client-Financed Acquisition in 30 days ($100M Leads), and the 2× cash rule ($100M Money Models / the Gym Launch 30-Day Cash Model). Planning tool, not accounting or financial advice.
